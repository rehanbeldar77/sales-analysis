# Interview preparation: Mobile Sales Dashboard

## 1. Explain your project in 60 seconds
“I created a Power BI dashboard to explore mobile sales performance. It includes KPI cards for sales, quantity, transactions and an average measure, plus analysis by city, month/day, brand, model, payment method and customer ratings. I added slicers to let users filter the report and compare segments. I would validate the measure definitions and data quality before using the findings for business decisions.”

## 2. Questions to practise

1. What business problem does this dashboard solve?
2. What is the difference between a measure and a calculated column in Power BI?
3. Why would you use `DIVIDE()` instead of `/` in DAX?
4. What is the difference between `SUM()` and `SUMX()`?
5. How do slicers affect visuals on a report page?
6. What is a star schema, and why is it useful?
7. Why should a Date table be used for time intelligence?
8. How would you validate total sales against the source data?
9. How would you handle missing city or rating values?
10. What would you improve if you had another day to work on this dashboard?

## 3. Be ready to explain your own work

Before an interview, make sure you can demonstrate:
- Where the data came from and how it was loaded.
- What cleaning steps you personally performed.
- The exact formula behind every KPI.
- How relationships and filter context work in your model.
- One real finding from the report, with its value and business implication.
- One limitation and one improvement.

Never claim a transformation, formula or result you did not personally verify.
